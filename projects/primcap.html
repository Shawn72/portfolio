What it does

01

Widget-level dashboard control

Visibility control goes beyond hiding whole sidebar sections — individual stat cards and charts on the staff home dashboard can be configured on or off per user, so a dashboard genuinely fits the role viewing it.

02

Fail-closed menu registration

Every new top-level sidebar section is deliberately hidden from everyone until explicitly seeded into the visibility table — new features can't accidentally leak to staff who shouldn't see them yet.

03

Line-based access model

Staff visibility is scoped by line number rather than a flat role field, matching how the organization actually assigns responsibilities.
