Ninjax Inventory & POS

End-to-end client walkthrough: provision the business, activate the subscription, configure stock and staff, sell through the till, review sales and expenses, and monitor Profit & Loss.

Subscription Admin → Clients

1. Provision the business

Create or select the client, assign the correct plan, confirm subscription status and activation-key state, then use the available client actions for support or account administration.
Subscription Admin → Plans

2. Configure the plan

Review the plan, price, and billing cycle before assigning it to the client.
Client Portal → Dashboard

3. Activate or renew

The client sees the current plan, remaining days, payment references, history, and activation key used to unlock subscribed POS functionality.
Inventory/POS → Dashboard

4. Review operations

Start with item totals, categories, stock warnings, and recent stock activity.
Inventory → Items

5. Create and maintain items

Add or edit product name, SKU/barcode, category, selling price, cost, and live quantity.
Inventory → Photos & Features

6. Add product media and features

Upload the main image and gallery views, then capture structured features/specifications where required.
Sales & Till → Stores

7. Configure store locations

Create or verify outlets so sales and finance reporting can be viewed by location or consolidated.
Administration → Roles & Rights

8. Define staff permissions

Assign permissions such as POS selling, item management, accounting access, and user administration to the appropriate roles.
Administration → Users

9. Create staff users

Register staff, assign roles, review account status, and deactivate users when access is no longer required.
Sales & Till → Till

10. Sell at the till

Scan or enter the SKU, confirm the cart, choose Cash, Card, M-Pesa, or On Account/Credit, and complete the transaction.
Sales & Till → Sales

11. Review sales

Filter sales by store, date, and status, then export results to Excel or PDF when needed.
Finance → Expenses

12. Record expenses

Capture categorized business expenses so they feed directly into finance reporting.
Finance → Profit & Loss

13. Review business performance

Review revenue, categorized expenses, and net income for the selected period and store.
Subscription Admin → Support

14. Support and renew

Use the client support thread for ongoing assistance. The client returns to the Client Portal for renewal, completing the full lifecycle from provisioning to continued operation.
