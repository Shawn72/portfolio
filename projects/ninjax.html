Ninjax — inventory & POS licensing platform

A subscription-licensed inventory and point-of-sale system for retail businesses, built as three connected surfaces: an inventory/POS admin app each business runs day to day, a client portal for managing their subscription, and a separate internal console Ninjax uses to license and support every business on the platform.

What it does

01

Three surfaces, one licensing model

The inventory/POS app, the client's own subscription portal, and Ninjax's internal admin are separate apps with separate audiences, but all key off the same subscription and activation-key state — a lapsed subscription locks premium POS features until renewed.

02

Provisioned, not self-serve

New clients are added directly by Ninjax through the subscription admin console — there's no public sign-up flow, keeping onboarding and plan assignment fully controlled.

03

Accounting-grade P&L from POS activity

Sales revenue and recorded expenses are both coded to accounting categories (e.g. 4000 Sales Revenue, 6200 Utilities), so the Profit & Loss report is a direct roll-up of real till and expense activity rather than a separate manual entry.

04

Permission-string role model

Rights like items.manage, pos.sell, and accounting.manage are assigned per role rather than hardcoded to a role name, so a business can shape what "Senior Staff" or "Staff" can actually touch.

05

Multi-location till and reporting

Sales, expenses, and stock all carry a store location, so a business running several outlets gets both per-store and consolidated views without separate installs.
